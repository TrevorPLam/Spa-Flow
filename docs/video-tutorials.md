# Spa-Flow Video Tutorial Scripts

**Last Updated:** May 22, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

This document contains scripts for video tutorials covering common Spa-Flow workflows. These scripts can be used to create training videos for new staff members.

---

## Tutorial 1: Check-In Process

**Duration:** 5-7 minutes  
**Target Audience:** New staff members

### Script

**[Opening Scene: Spa-Flow login page]**

**Narrator:** Welcome to the Spa-Flow check-in tutorial. In this video, you'll learn how to check in a client and assign them a locker or room.

**[Scene: Staff member logs in]**

**Narrator:** First, log in with your username and password. If this is your first time, you'll be prompted to change your password.

**[Scene: Dashboard appears]**

**Narrator:** After logging in, you'll see the Dashboard. This shows current occupancy, today's revenue, and active sessions. Let's start a check-in.

**[Scene: Navigate to Check-In page]**

**Narrator:** Click "Check-In" in the navigation menu to begin the check-in process.

**[Scene: Search for client]**

**Narrator:** First, search for your client. You can search by name, email, phone number, or member ID. If the client doesn't exist, click "Create New Client" to add them to the system.

**[Scene: Client selected]**

**Narrator:** Once you've selected the client, choose the resource type - either a locker or a room.

**[Scene: Select resource type]**

**Narrator:** For this example, let's assign a locker. You'll see all available lockers shown in green.

**[Scene: Select specific locker]**

**Narrator:** Click on an available locker to select it. You'll see the locker number and location.

**[Scene: Process payment]**

**Narrator:** The system calculates the price automatically. Process the payment through Square, cash, or card. For Square payments, follow the on-screen prompts on the terminal.

**[Scene: Confirm assignment]**

**Narrator:** Review the assignment details and click "Confirm Check-In." The locker is now assigned to the client.

**[Scene: Locker status changes to occupied]**

**Narrator:** Notice the locker status has changed from green to red, indicating it's now occupied.

**[Closing Scene: Summary]**

**Narrator:** That's the complete check-in process. Remember to release the locker when the client is done. For more information, refer to the user manual or quick reference guide.

## Tutorial 2: Client Management

**Duration:** 4-5 minutes  
**Target Audience:** New staff members

### Script

**[Opening Scene: Clients page]**

**Narrator:** Welcome to the client management tutorial. In this video, you'll learn how to create, search, and manage client profiles.

**[Scene: Navigate to Clients page]**

**Narrator:** Click "Clients" in the navigation menu to access client management.

**[Scene: Search for existing client]**

**Narrator:** To find an existing client, use the search bar. Type in their name, email, phone, or member ID. Results appear as you type.

**[Scene: Click on client]**

**Narrator:** Click on a client's name to view their profile. You'll see their contact information, membership status, rental history, and transaction history.

**[Scene: Create new client]**

**Narrator:** To create a new client, click "New Client." Fill in the required fields: first name, last name, email, phone number, and date of birth. Address and document number are optional.

**[Scene: Save client]**

**Narrator:** Click "Create Client" to save the profile. The client is now in the system.

**[Scene: Add membership]**

**Narrator:** To add a membership, navigate to the client's profile and click "Add Membership." Choose between one-time or six-month membership, then process the payment.

**[Scene: Update client information]**

**Narrator:** To update client information, click "Edit" on their profile, make changes, and click "Save Changes."

**[Closing Scene: Summary]**

**Narrator:** That covers client management. Always verify client information before saving, and keep records accurate for reporting purposes.

## Tutorial 3: Resource Management

**Duration:** 6-8 minutes  
**Target Audience:** New staff members

### Script

**[Opening Scene: Lockers page]**

**Narrator:** Welcome to the resource management tutorial. In this video, you'll learn how to manage lockers and rooms, including assignment, release, renewal, and extension.

**[Scene: Navigate to Lockers page]**

**Narrator:** Click "Lockers" in the navigation menu to view all lockers. Green lockers are available, red are occupied, and yellow are reserved.

**[Scene: Assign locker]**

**Narrator:** To assign a locker, click on an available one, select a client, and click "Assign." Process the payment if required.

**[Scene: Release locker]**

**Narrator:** When a client is done, click on the occupied locker and click "Release." Confirm the release to make it available again.

**[Scene: Renew session]**

**Narrator:** To renew a session for 6 hours, click on the occupied resource and click "Renew." Process the payment for the extension.

**[Scene: Extend session]**

**Narrator:** To add 2 hours to a session, click "Extend" instead. This is useful for short extensions.

**[Scene: Bulk release]**

**Narrator:** To release multiple lockers at once, select them using checkboxes and click "Bulk Release."

**[Scene: Navigate to Rooms page]**

**Narrator:** The same process applies to rooms. Navigate to the Rooms page and follow the same steps for assignment, release, renewal, and extension.

**[Closing Scene: Summary]**

**Narrator:** That covers resource management. Remember to release resources promptly when clients finish to keep availability accurate.

## Tutorial 4: Waitlist Management

**Duration:** 4-5 minutes  
**Target Audience:** New staff members

### Script

**[Opening Scene: Waitlist page]**

**Narrator:** Welcome to the waitlist management tutorial. In this video, you'll learn how to manage the waitlist when rooms are fully occupied.

**[Scene: Explain waitlist purpose]**

**Narrator:** When all rooms are occupied, clients can join the waitlist. The system automatically assigns rooms as they become available and sends SMS notifications.

**[Scene: Add client to waitlist]**

**Narrator:** To add a client to the waitlist, click "Add to Waitlist." Search for or create a client, then click "Add to Waitlist."

**[Scene: View waitlist position]**

**Narrator:** The waitlist shows each client's position, contact information, time added, and status. Clients are served in order.

**[Scene: Room becomes available]**

**Narrator:** When a room becomes available, the client at the top of the waitlist receives an SMS notification if Twilio is configured.

**[Scene: Confirm assignment]**

**Narrator:** Navigate to the Waitlist page and click "Confirm" next to the client's name. The client has 15 minutes to confirm the assignment.

**[Scene: Assignment expires]**

**Narrator:** If the client doesn't confirm within 15 minutes, the assignment expires and automatically goes to the next person on the waitlist.

**[Scene: Remove from waitlist]**

**Narrator:** To remove a client from the waitlist, click "Remove" next to their name and confirm.

**[Closing Scene: Summary]**

**Narrator:** That covers waitlist management. The system handles most of the process automatically, but you need to confirm assignments promptly when rooms become available.

## Tutorial 5: Troubleshooting Common Issues

**Duration:** 5-6 minutes  
**Target Audience:** All staff members

### Script

**[Opening Scene: Troubleshooting overview]**

**Narrator:** Welcome to the troubleshooting tutorial. In this video, you'll learn how to resolve common issues you may encounter while using Spa-Flow.

**[Scene: Page not loading]**

**Narrator:** If a page isn't loading, first try refreshing the page by pressing F5 on Windows or Command+R on Mac. Check your internet connection if the issue persists.

**[Scene: Can't log in]**

**Narrator:** If you can't log in, verify your username and password. If you've had 5 failed attempts, your account is locked for 15 minutes. Contact a manager to unlock your account.

**[Scene: Payment failed]**

**Narrator:** If a payment fails, check the Square terminal connection and try again. Ensure the card is valid and has sufficient funds. For cash payments, verify the amount before confirming.

**[Scene: Resource not available]**

**Narrator:** If a resource isn't available, check the waitlist. Add the client to the waitlist if they're willing to wait. The system will automatically assign when a resource becomes available.

**[Scene: Client not found]**

**Narrator:** If you can't find a client, try different search terms (name, email, phone). If they're truly not in the system, create a new client profile.

**[Scene: System slow]**

**Narrator:** If the system is slow, check your internet connection. Close other browser tabs that might be using bandwidth. If the issue persists, contact IT support.

**[Scene: Account locked out]**

**Narrator:** If your account is locked out due to too many failed login attempts, wait 15 minutes for the automatic unlock, or contact a manager to unlock it immediately.

**[Closing Scene: Summary]**

**Narrator:** For more detailed troubleshooting information, refer to the troubleshooting guide. If you encounter an issue not covered here, contact your manager or IT support.

## Production Notes

### Recording Guidelines

- Use screen recording software (e.g., OBS, Loom, Camtasia)
- Record at 1080p resolution or higher
- Use a clear, professional voice
- Speak at a moderate pace
- Use mouse highlighting to draw attention to important elements
- Include captions for accessibility

### Editing Guidelines

- Add intro and outro slides with Spa-Flow branding
- Use consistent transitions between scenes
- Add text overlays for key terms
- Include chapter markers for easy navigation
- Keep videos under 10 minutes for better engagement
- Export in MP4 format for compatibility

### Distribution

- Upload to company video platform (e.g., YouTube, Vimeo internal)
- Add to staff onboarding checklist
- Link from user manual and quick reference guide
- Update videos when major UI changes occur
